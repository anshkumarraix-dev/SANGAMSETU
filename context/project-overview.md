# SangamSetu (संगमसेतु) — Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
**SangamSetu (संगमसेतु)** is the Government of India's statutory innovation procurement portal operated under the **Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)**, Ministry of Commerce & Industry.

The platform bridges Central and State Ministries, Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs), and municipal bodies with DPIIT-recognized startups to solve national technology challenges under **General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 Rules 149 & 161(iv)**.

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Statutory GFR Exemption Enforcement**: Automatic relaxation of prior turnover and prior experience criteria for DPIIT-recognized entities with verified technical capability.
2. **Explainable AI Evaluation Algorithm**: Transparent 7-dimension multi-criteria scoring algorithm providing instant technical scorecards (0–100) and G1 (Quality) & G2 (Cost) category rosters.
3. **STQC / NABL Lab Benchmarking**: Rigorous hardware, firmware, and code benchmarking by accredited government test labs before any financial sanction.
4. **Electronic PFMS Direct Bank Transfer (DBT)**: Milestone-linked 40-30-30 financial disbursement integrated with Public Financial Management System (PFMS) and automated GeM scale-out transition.

## 3. Statutory & Policy Framework
- **General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017**: Rule 149 (Government e-Marketplace) and Rule 161(iv) (Relaxation of Prior Turnover/Experience for Startups).
- **Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order 2017**: Class-I & Class-II local supplier preference.
- **GeM Special Direct Procurement Provision**: Fast-track scale-out contracting upon successful pilot completion without re-tendering.
